Short version

This website does not use cookies, analytics, tracking, advertising, embedded third-party content or forms. I do not collect any personal data myself.

Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted by Render Services, Inc. (San Francisco, USA) and served through its content delivery network, which is provided by Cloudflare, Inc. When you open the site, your browser automatically sends technical data to these servers, such as your IP address, the date and time, the requested page, and browser information. This data is processed to deliver the site and to keep it secure and stable. I do not use these logs to identify visitors.

Because the provider is based in the USA, this data may be processed there. Render Services, Inc. is certified under the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework, which the Swiss Federal Council recognises as providing adequate data protection for certified recipients.

No cookies, no storage

This site does not set cookies and does not store anything in your browser (no localStorage or similar). It reads your browser's language setting to choose EN or DE, and carries your choice between pages through the link itself (?lang=de).

Fonts and scripts

All fonts and scripts are served from this website itself. Nothing is loaded from third-party servers, so no data is sent to other companies when you visit.

Email

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I only use it to reply and do not pass it on. I delete messages when they are no longer needed. My email is provided by Swisscom (bluewin.ch).

Links to other sites

This site links to GitHub and YouTube. Nothing is transmitted to them until you click a link. After that, their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection you can ask me whether I hold personal data about you, and request its correction or deletion. Just email me. You can also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC): edoeb.admin.ch
